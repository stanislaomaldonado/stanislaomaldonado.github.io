01
Research laboratory

Behavioral & Experimental Economics Research Lab (BEER Lab)

I am the founder and director of BEER Lab at Tecnológico de Monterrey. The laboratory studies how people make decisions through laboratory, field and online experiments, bringing together researchers, instructors and students.

Its activities connect research with classroom experiments and hands-on training in experimental methods. The lab provides resources for running studies, a collection of teaching games, a working paper series and a Diploma in Experimental Economics.

02
Student initiative

NudgeTEC

I serve as Faculty Advisor to NudgeTEC, an initiative of the Economics Student Society (SELEC) and BEER Lab at Tecnológico de Monterrey. It brings students together to use economics and psychology to improve well-being.

Through readings, workshops and experimental design, students learn to develop evidence-based proposals and collaborate with communities and organizations. NudgeTEC also co-organizes the Diploma in Experimental Economics with BEER Lab.

03
Learning platform

Pukllay

I direct Pukllay, a joint project of LACEA BRAIN and Tecnológico de Monterrey through BEER Lab. It is a free, accessible platform that brings behavioral and experimental economics to teachers and students across Latin America through classroom games.

Participants make decisions, observe the outcomes and discuss the economic mechanisms with their instructor. Games can be used in the laboratory, in class or remotely, with interfaces, instructions and teaching guides in English, Spanish and Portuguese. The project broadens access to experimental teaching without requiring each university to develop its own platform.
