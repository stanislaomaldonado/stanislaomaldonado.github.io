About me

Research, teaching and public policy.

I am a research professor of economics at Tecnológico de Monterrey in Mexico. My research and teaching span development economics, political economy and their links with behavioral economics.

I am the co-founder and director of the Behavioral & Experimental Economics Research Lab (BEER Lab) at Tecnológico de Monterrey, where we connect research, teaching and training in behavioral and experimental economics.

I received my Ph.D. in Agricultural and Resource Economics from the University of California, Berkeley. I previously taught at Universidad del Pacífico in Lima and Universidad del Rosario in Bogotá.

Before my doctoral studies, I worked as a Junior Professional Associate in the World Bank’s Human Development Department for Latin America and the Caribbean in Washington, D.C.

I have worked as a consultant for multilateral organizations including the World Bank, the Inter-American Development Bank, and CAF — Development Bank of Latin America and the Caribbean, as well as international nonprofit organizations such as the American Cancer Society.

In Peru, I have advised the Congress of the Republic of Peru, the Ministry of Development and Social Inclusion, the Ministry of Economy and Finance, the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Labor and Employment Promotion, and the Ministry of Production.

In Colombia, I have advised the National Planning Department and the Special Administrative Unit of the Public Employment Service.

I have also taught professional training courses for the Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab (J-PAL), Innovations for Poverty Action (IPA), and the Center for Effective Global Action (CEGA).

I am a network co-director of LACEA BRAIN (Behavioral Insights Network), the behavioral and experimental economics research network of the Latin American and Caribbean Economic Association (LACEA). I also co-organize the Workshop of Experimental and Behavioral Economics of the Americas (WEBEA).

My academic service includes the Advisory Committee for the University Extension Program in Data Science, run by Peru’s National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI), and the Scientific Committee for the RIDGE Summer School in Economics, organized by the Research Institute for Development, Growth and Economics (RIDGE).

I also direct Pukllay, a joint project of LACEA BRAIN and Tecnológico de Monterrey through BEER Lab that brings behavioral and experimental economics to classrooms across Latin America through free online games.

I created the Spanish-language MOOC Behavioral Economics for Effective Public Management on edX. I also share reflections on economics and politics on Substack.
